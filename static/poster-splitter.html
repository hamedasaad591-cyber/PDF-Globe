How to print a large poster on A4 paper

A tiled poster is a large design divided into smaller printable sheets. For example, an A1 poster can be divided across several A4 pages, printed on a regular home or office printer, and assembled into one larger display. This tool calculates the sheet grid and includes an adjustable overlap so adjacent pages share part of the image.

Choose the right overlap and print scale

An overlap of around 5–10 mm is a practical starting point for many projects, but the best value depends on your printer and how you plan to join the sheets. Always print at Actual Size or 100%. Printer settings such as “Fit”, “Shrink oversized pages” or “Scale to printable area” can change the final dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool upload my file?

No. Image decoding, PDF page rendering and poster generation happen in your browser. The page loads its PDF libraries from a public CDN, but the selected document is not sent to PDF Globe's server.

Can I upload a multi-page PDF?

Yes, but this first release uses only page 1 of the uploaded PDF. Support for choosing other pages can be added in a later release.

Why are there white edges on some sheets?

White space can come from printer margins or from the selected “Fit entire image” option. It can also appear on the final row or column because the poster edge does not always line up exactly with a full sheet.

What resolution should I choose?

150 DPI is a reasonable starting point for many large posters. Choose 200 DPI when the source image has enough detail and your browser can handle the larger output.

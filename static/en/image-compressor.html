Frequently asked questions

Which image formats are supported?

Input supports JPG, JPEG, JFIF, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, HEIF, BMP, GIF, TIFF, ICO, TGA, PPM, PGM, PBM and PCX. Output supports JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, BMP, GIF, TIFF, ICO and PDF.

Which format should I choose?

WebP often produces smaller web images. JPG is suitable for photographs, while PNG is useful for graphics that need transparency or crisp edges.

Does compression affect image quality?

JPG and WebP compression may reduce some details depending on the chosen quality. Keep the original when you need maximum quality.

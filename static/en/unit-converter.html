Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals exactly 1,000 grams.

Does one cup weigh the same for every ingredient?

No. A cup measures volume while grams measure mass. Weight changes with ingredient density and packing. This tool uses approximate densities for common ingredients.

Are my values sent to a server?

No. Unit conversions and recipe scaling run locally in your browser. No file upload or account is required.
